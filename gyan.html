About

I work at the point where design decisions turn into shipped code. Most of my time goes into products used by small, focused teams — tools that need to disappear into someone's workflow rather than demand attention.

Before this I spent three years building design systems for an early-stage fintech, which is where I learned that consistency is a feature, not a constraint. These days I take on a small number of projects at a time, usually end to end: research, interface, and the front-end build.
